## Why no further deploy-fix work was dispatched this pass

Per protocol: stop rather than attempt a 3rd try of an identical approach after two consecutive failures.
"Reset the live checkout onto origin/main" has already been independently *claimed* twice
(`UMR171945-BLK06` on 2026-08-08, `UMR-20260813-101142-5d24` on 2026-08-13) and both regressed within
days/hours, because nothing stops the next worker task from checking out its own branch directly into
`/opt/veridian/scripts`. A 3rd identical "just reset it again" attempt would very likely regress the same
way again. The real missing piece is structural -- something must either stop worker tasks from leaving
`/opt/veridian/scripts` on a non-main branch, or make the governor tick loop refuse to run off a non-main
HEAD -- and that is real, separately-scoped remaining work, tracked via the 4 issue-tracker writes above
for a dedicated follow-up dispatch.
